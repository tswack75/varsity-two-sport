# Exercise demonstration coverage

All 23 exercises in the shipped Blue, Maize, White and Home programs have
offline technique notes and original front/back SVG muscle schematics. Yellow
marks primary muscles, teal marks secondary muscles, and the guide lists both
groups in text. Custom workout choices share the same catalog.

The app links to the publisher's page, which may contain a video, animation
or illustrated demonstration. No third-party media is embedded, downloaded,
or redistributed by Varsity. The app makes no claim of a redistribution
license. Links require a network connection. When a publisher changes a page,
the local guide still works.

Broad legacy exercises show the specific equipment in the linked demo.
Explicit catalog variants use separate history when the older loading
convention is uncertain. Earlier user-written substitutions outside the
catalog show a local fallback message. No alternate media is substituted.
